# Life Revelations

Sometimes in life, you just have to follow your instincts even though you're not sure. You don't have to figure it out before you can move forward. Just do what is in your mind and figure it out as you go. Because if you wait to think about everything, you might not even end up doing anything. And most people would say, the fastest way to figure it out is on the way. You don't wait before you move because you won't have enough data to even move forward. Everybody figured it out while going. Nobody figured it out before they stepped.

Believe me when I say this: there is nothing beneficial in life about being nonchalant. Even wealthy people are not nonchalant. Only people with low self-esteem, trying to cope and give themselves a false sense of security, pretend not to care, ultimately ruining their lives in the process. And if you are a guy who believes that acting nonchalant will make girls take you more seriously, I am telling you the truth: any girl who has ambition and direction in life will not be attracted to a nonchalant man. 

Only girls who are lost, who also struggle with low self-esteem, would want to spend the rest of their lives with someone like that. Only someone with childhood complexes and unresolved issues, ready to settle for the bare minimum, would end up with a nonchalant person. There is a profound difference between being nonchalant and being genuinely confident. Confidence is what actually attracts people of quality to your life. A confident person will say no when they need to, whereas a nonchalant person will always default to "I don't care" as a defense mechanism. They are merely pretending not to care, and by doing so, they chase away good people who could genuinely help them, ultimately attracting only those who will ruin their lives. 

So, be authentic, work on yourself, and learn to cultivate real confidence. If something is not okay for you, walk away. If something is okay for you, embrace it. You do not have to force yourself to fit into a toxic trend. At the end of the day, you must allow yourself to live as your best self, rather than pretending to be someone you are not just to impress others. Stand firm in who you are, and whoever cannot accept you for your genuine self can simply walk away; because true confidence means never having to hide behind a mask of indifference.

Sometimes you just need to learn how to trust God. If there is an easier way, then follow it. Don't waste all your entire life stressing yourself. Don't force yourself to figure everything out or you'll forget to live. So live, live how you want to live. Trust God to help you. That is why the Holy Spirit is there. Depend on him. Don't focus on how other people who didn't have the Holy Spirit figured it out. Figure it out for yourself. Allow the Holy Spirit to show you. Don't feel guilty because you have the Holy Spirit. Don't feel guilty because other people didn't maximize the advantages they have through Jesus. Allow yourself to depend on God.
